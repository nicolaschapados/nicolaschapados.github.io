I am a machine-learning researcher who keeps building companies around the research. Today I am Co-Founder and Chief Scientist of [Nera Software](https://www.nera.software), a Montréal startup currently in stealth mode. I also teach and supervise as Adjunct Professor of Applied Mathematics at Polytechnique Montréal, and I am an Associate Industry Member of [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en/directory/nicolas-chapados), the Québec AI institute.

## Before

From 2021 to 2025 I was Vice-President of Research at ServiceNow, leading the research organization that came out of the acquisition of Element AI, which I had co-founded in 2016 and where I was Chief Science Officer. Our group worked on large language models, code models, time-series foundation models and, increasingly, agents that carry out enterprise work; several of those results are on the [publications page](/en/publications/).

Before that I co-founded Imagia, applying deep learning to medical imaging, and in 2001, while still writing my thesis, [ApSTAT Technologies](https://apstat.com/author/nicolas-chapados/) with Yoshua Bengio, to transfer machine-learning research into insurance, supply-chain planning, forecasting and finance. In 2012 I co-founded [Chapados Couture Capital](https://www.chapados-couture.com) with Éric-Paul Couture; it operated as a quantitative portfolio manager from 2013 to 2025 and is now a private holding company. Earlier, at Nortel Networks, I worked on speech recognition and natural-language understanding.

## Research

I care about learning systems that make good decisions: time-series forecasting, from N-BEATS and TACTiS to foundation models such as Lag-Llama; decision-making under uncertainty in finance and operations; AI for healthcare; and, most recently, benchmarks and methods for AI agents on knowledge work. I studied computer engineering at McGill (B.Eng., 1995) and computer science at Université de Montréal (M.Sc. 2000, Ph.D. 2009) under Yoshua Bengio, and I hold the CFA designation.

Beyond papers, I supervise graduate students at Polytechnique Montréal and Mila, review for the main machine-learning venues, and speak regularly to industry, regulators and the public about what AI can and cannot yet do. The full record is in my [academic CV](/cv.pdf).
