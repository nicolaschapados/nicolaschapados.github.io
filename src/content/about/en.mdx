I’m Nicolas Chapados, a machine-learning researcher and entrepreneur based in Montréal.

One of my first jobs, as a teenager, was programming the PC version of [*Wrath of the Demon*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wrath_of_the_Demon). Everything was written by hand in 80x86 assembly. I spent a lot of time trying to move bytes through video memory fast enough to keep the game running smoothly. We shipped on floppy disks and CDs, and fixing bugs sometimes required patching code in memory after the disks had already been copied.

Since then, my work has taken me through speech recognition, quantitative finance, medical imaging and enterprise AI. I’ve remained interested in how research becomes something people can use, which has often meant building a company as well as writing software.

## What I’m working on

Much of my research concerns learning and decision-making under uncertainty. How can we make useful forecasts when observations are incomplete, circumstances change and the past is an imperfect guide? How should those forecasts inform what we do next? These questions connect my work on time series, including [N-BEATS](/en/publications/#oreshkin2019n) and [TACTiS](/en/publications/#pmlr-v162-drouin22a), with my experience in investment management and business forecasting.

More recently, I’ve been working on AI agents: how to train them to carry out increasingly complex tasks, evaluate their abilities, and make their behaviour more reliable. I’m interested in both improving what they can do and understanding when we have good reasons to trust them. That means examining how they learn, the tools and feedback they receive, and whether our evaluations capture the work we actually need them to perform.

I pursue part of this work as co-founder and Chief Scientist of [Nera Software](https://www.nera.software). We’re building the system of record for verified intent, helping teams express their decisions and requirements in forms that can guide coding agents and be used to check their work.

## How I got here

I studied computer engineering at [McGill](https://www.mcgill.ca/), then completed my master’s and doctorate in computer science at [Université de Montréal](https://www.umontreal.ca/) under [Yoshua Bengio](https://yoshuabengio.org/en). I worked on speech recognition and natural-language understanding at [Nortel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nortel), and co-founded [ApSTAT](https://apstat.com/), [Chapados Couture Capital](https://www.chapados-couture.com/) and [Imagia](https://web.archive.org/web/20220814085123/https://imagia.com/), applying machine learning to forecasting, investment management and medical imaging.

In 2016, I co-founded [Element AI](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Element_AI), where I was Chief Science Officer. Following its acquisition, I led [AI research](https://www.servicenow.com/research/) at [ServiceNow](https://www.servicenow.com/) from 2021 to 2025.

I’m also an Adjunct Professor of Applied Mathematics at [Polytechnique Montréal](https://www.polymtl.ca/expertises/en/chapados-nicolas) and an Associate Industry Member of [Mila](https://mila.quebec/en/directory/nicolas-chapados). Research, graduate supervision and building things remain closely connected parts of my work. Sometimes an application exposes a question we don’t know how to answer yet. Those are often the questions I want to spend more time on.
