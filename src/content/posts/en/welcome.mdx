This site gathers three things that used to live in different places: a biography I can point people to, the complete list of my [publications](/en/publications/) with archived PDFs, and a place to write.

The publication list is not maintained by hand. It is generated from the same BibTeX file as my academic CV, so when the CV changes, one command refreshes the site. Titles and venues stay in their original language; the rest of the site exists in English and in French.

The blog will carry popularizations of research topics I am working on, mostly around forecasting, decision-making under uncertainty and AI agents for enterprise work. Posts may appear in one language first; the other version follows when it exists. There are no comments here, but every post ends with links to discuss it on Bluesky, X or LinkedIn, and there is an [RSS feed](/en/rss.xml).
