# chapados.ca is the canonical domain; other domains redirect to it

The owner holds chapados.ca and chapadosresearch.org (both at Gandi) and portfolioparadigms.com (at Wild West Domains, currently a WordPress.com blog), and may register more later. GitHub Pages serves a single custom domain per site, so aliases are impossible; every other domain 301-redirects to chapados.ca via registrar-level web forwarding. chapados.ca was chosen because it is the shortest, matches the owner's email domain, and is person-first rather than tied to a research or portfolio framing that may not outlive the site.

## Consequences

- Redirecting portfolioparadigms.com takes the existing WordPress.com blog offline; whether its posts migrate into the new blog is a separate decision.
- Absolute URLs (RSS, Open Graph, sitemap) use https://chapados.ca; www.chapados.ca redirects to the apex.
